## What it does

MeetPulse Desktop turns your meetings into searchable memory. It captures both your system audio and your microphone — works with Meet, Zoom, Teams or a phone call — with no bots or extensions joining the call.

- **Live transcription**: local Parakeet and Whisper (Whisper covers ~100 languages), or optional cloud transcription with Gemini and MAI-Transcribe.
- **Local-first AI**: Qwen 3.5 2B and Gemma 4 E4B run summaries, chat and coach fully offline. Nothing leaves your machine.
- **Optional cloud via Vercel AI Gateway**: a single API key of yours covers transcription, analysis and chat, with ZDR / No-Training models depending on the provider.
- **Automatic insights**: summary, decisions, action items with owners, topics and risks — every point links to the exact minute.
- **Meety**: an assistant that answers questions about one meeting or a client's whole history, with clickable citations.
- **Local semantic search** (Ctrl+K): find what was said, not just what you typed.
- **PDF reports**: summary and transcript, generated on your machine.
- **Real privacy control**: you choose per task what leaves your PC (all local / text only to the cloud / audio and text to the cloud).
- Spanish and English UI.

## Platform

Windows 10/11 (64-bit) · macOS and Linux coming soon. Free, with no meeting or hour limits.

## Install

1. Download the Windows installer (download link above).
2. Open MeetPulse and follow the welcome flow: audio permissions and model download.
3. Hit **Record** on the Home screen.

Cloud models require your own Vercel AI Gateway API key; the app doesn't ship one.

> Installers are not digitally signed yet. If Windows SmartScreen warns you, click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
